# Getting started

RaioPDF opens a PDF from your computer and gives you what you need to read,
organize, edit, and prepare it — all on your own machine, with no account to
create.

## Open a PDF

There are two ways:

1. **Drag a PDF onto the window.**
2. Click **Open a PDF** and choose one from your computer.

The start screen shows "Open a PDF to get started." Once you're working, the
**Open** button in the toolbar brings up another file at any time.

If a file won't open, RaioPDF offers to **repair** it — some PDFs are built in
unusual ways, and a repair pass usually fixes them.

## Find your way around

- The **toolbar** across the top handles the basics: open, save, print, moving
  page to page, zoom, and search.
- The panel on the **left** shows page thumbnails and the document's bookmarks,
  for jumping to a page or reordering pages.
- The **Tools** panel on the right is grouped into **Edit**, **Annotate**,
  **Organize**, and **Legal**, with **Make Searchable (OCR)** on its own.
  **Edit** changes the document's real content (Edit Text, page numbers,
  watermarks); **Annotate** layers comments and markup on top of it.
- **Select** is your normal reading mode — the plain pointer. When you're done
  with any tool, click it again or press **Esc** to return to Select.

Scroll inside the bookmark list to browse a long outline. The document stays
on its current page while the list moves, including at the list's ends.

## Printing

Click **Print** in the toolbar, choose a printer, then choose pages and copies.
**Paper size** and **Paper source** list the choices reported by that printer.
Paper source may be called a tray or drawer in the printer's own software.
Leave either field at **Printer default** to use its normal setting. A printer
with no advertised sources has only the default source choice.

Changing printers clears paper choices. While choices load, or if RaioPDF cannot
read them, you can still print with the printer's defaults. macOS also offers
sides and orientation; Windows uses the printer's existing defaults for those settings.
On Windows, choosing a paper size or source fits each visible page proportionally
inside that paper's printable area. Large custom forms may exceed the supported
rendering limit. Printer settings apply to this job and do not change saved
printer preferences.

Windows custom printing reports an error before rendering pages with malformed
or unsupported page geometry. A file that opens in the viewer may still need
its page boxes repaired before custom printing.

If an explicit choice cannot be applied, RaioPDF reports the problem. After a
job has started, some pages may already have printed; check the output before
retrying.

## Saving your work

Tools change the document you have open; nothing is written to your disk until
you **Save**. Use **Save As** to write the changed version to a new file and
keep the original untouched.
